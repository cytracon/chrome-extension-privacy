Privacy Policy — Cytracon Authenticator

Publisher: Cytracon Webservices · bb@cytracon.com · 17 August 2026

Cytracon Authenticator generates TOTP codes locally. Account secrets stay in Chrome extension storage on your device.

If you enable backup, the extension writes an encrypted JSON file to the hidden app-data folder of your Google Drive. No Cytracon server receives secrets.

Current tab host is used only to suggest the matching account. Data is not sold or used for advertising. No remote code.
