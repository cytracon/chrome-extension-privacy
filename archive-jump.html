Privacy Policy — Archive Jump

Publisher: Cytracon Webservices · bb@cytracon.com · 17 August 2026

Archive Jump opens archive.today / Wayback snapshots of the current page. It runs only in your browser.

It reads the current tab URL to build archive links. It does not send browsing history to Cytracon. Settings are stored in Chrome local storage. No remote code, no analytics, no sale of data.

Host access to http/https is used only to open or probe archive mirrors and the Wayback Machine for the page you choose.
