Privacy Policy — Cytracon Pinned Tabs

Publisher: Cytracon Webservices · bb@cytracon.com · 17 August 2026

This extension stores pinned-tab profiles (URLs and window layout) in Chrome storage. Optional sync writes that profile data to your own Google Drive or a local native host you install.

Tab URLs are used only to reopen your saved profiles. No Cytracon server receives the list. No advertising, no remote code, no sale of data.
