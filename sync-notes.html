Privacy Policy — Sync-Notes

Publisher: Cytracon Webservices · bb@cytracon.com · 17 August 2026

Sync-Notes stores the notes you write in Chrome extension storage. Export downloads a JSON file to your computer.

Selected text is saved only when you use the keyboard shortcut. Nothing is sent to a Cytracon server. No remote code, no advertising, no sale of data.
