Privacy Policy — Cytracon Session Isolator

Publisher: Cytracon Webservices · bb@cytracon.com · 17 August 2026

The extension isolates cookies and web storage per tab so named sessions (for example WhatsApp Web) do not leak into other tabs. Isolation happens locally in the browser.

It needs broad host access to apply isolation on the sites you open. It does not upload cookies or page content to Cytracon. Settings stay in Chrome storage. No remote code, no sale of data.
